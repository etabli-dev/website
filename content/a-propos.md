## Why a workbench

The tools began as things needed for the working day: running a quick R
analysis away from a desk, reading documents from a self-hosted archive, keeping
focused time honest. Each one is small, does one job, and is published so that
others can use, check and adapt it.

## Follow and contribute

- Source, issues and development builds: [github.com/etabli-dev](https://github.com/etabli-dev).
- Each repository has a `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Bug reports with the platform,
  build and steps to reproduce are the most useful contribution.
- Support development on [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/rabanheller/).
