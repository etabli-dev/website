## What it does

Table browses the tables and rows of one configured SeaTable base.

- Android shows the first 100 rows with complete column values.
- iOS shows up to 500 rows with a local text filter.

## Where it stands

A debug-signed Android development APK (v0.1.0) is on GitHub Releases. Editing
rows and choosing between several bases are not exposed yet, and further
pagination is deferred.
