## What it does

Nuage imports a CSV of URLs, locally or from a Nextcloud WebDAV path, and checks
each link in turn. Results are saved, shared as CSV or uploaded back to
Nextcloud.

## Where it stands

Builds from source. An Android development APK is prepared but not yet
published. The desktop preview has been built and checked on Linux. A contacts,
calendar and file browser are future work. Not affiliated with Nextcloud GmbH.
