## What it does

Projet lists your OpenProject projects and the work packages assigned to you.

- iOS also browses and searches a project's work packages and edits
  work-package subjects, with lock-version handling.
- Needs your own HTTPS server and API token.

## Where it stands

A debug-signed Android development APK (v0.1.0) is on GitHub Releases. Changing
assignments is not implemented.
